## Phase Goal

**ROADMAP `**Goal:**` line, verbatim:** The attacker faces an accuracy-drop target that scales with the leverage it actually has this round, resolved everywhere through one function, and the bottom rung is confirmed measurable before anything is built on it.

> **MVP_MODE note:** this Goal line is not in user-story form; the planner does not invent one. See Plan 01 for the full note.

<objective>
Measure whether the 0.02 bottom rung is bigger than the noise it will be measured against, record the answer, and raise the rung if it is not.

This is the gate half of Phase 1. `.planning/STATE.md` §Blockers and `.planning/ROADMAP.md` §Ordering Constraints both treat GOAL-06 as a gate rather than a task: if the bottom rung does not clear the per-round clean-counterfactual noise for every defense, the ladder is wrong and everything built on it inherits the error.

Purpose: at budget 1 the attacker is asked for a 2% accuracy drop. If the clean counterfactual itself swings by more than that between rounds, a budget-1 round's measured `drop` is mostly noise, and Phase 2's per-cell denominator and Phase 3's graded ASR will both be calibrated against noise for that cell. Research (`.planning/research/SUMMARY.md`, pitfall 6) already flags budget 1 as the least trustworthy rung, citing a documented counterfactual swing of sd ~0.012 under `union` mode. This plan measures it properly, per defense, under `single` mode.

Output: a committed measurement tool at `benchmark/noise_probe.py` with torch-free unit tests; a recorded verdict at `.planning/phases/01-budget-conditioned-target-ladder/01-NOISE-BASELINE.md`; and, if any defense fails, a raised bottom rung in all three declarations.
</objective>

## Flagged planner assumptions

1. **What "noise" means here.** `clean_reference_accuracy()` is deterministic *within* a round (same global weights, same defense, MNIST eval has no sampling). The quantity ROADMAP SC3 asks for is the variation **across repeated clean rounds** — the round-to-round swing of the clean counterfactual as the global model evolves and each defense's cross-round state (DeFL's Beta trust counts and critical-learning-period test, FLTrust's trust vector) accumulates. The probe therefore commits each clean round and records the next round's counterfactual, rather than calling `clean_reference_accuracy()` repeatedly inside one round, which would trivially return the identical value. If a reviewer intended within-round repeatability instead, that is a different measurement and this is the place to say so.
2. **The probe needs a real FL environment.** `build_ensemble` builds FLTrust's clean root set from the MNIST training set, so MNIST must be present or downloadable, and a Phase-1 checkpoint must exist or be trainable on CPU. This is captured as a `<precondition>` on Task 2 rather than assumed.
3. **The rung raise is arithmetic, not judgement.** `required_rung = ceil(3 * sd / 0.01) * 0.01` rounds up to the next whole percentage point of accuracy, matching the ladder's existing granularity (every rung is a multiple of 0.02). The executor does not choose a number.
